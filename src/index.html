    Engines of Independence

    Every solution is an engine. Every engine creates pressure.

    Build a town by choosing the engines underneath independence: power, freight, money, public works, press, policy, and civic trust. Coal can industrialize you. Banks can finance you. Newspapers can legitimize you. Saloons can fund a shadow economy. Taxes can build the public good or light the fuse. Every lever helps. Every lever bites.

    How the Engines Work
    1. Build access first: roads, housing, jobs, and basic civic support.
    2. Add engines: power, freight, finance, public works, press, policy, and social institutions.
    3. Watch the pressure meters: inflation, corruption, unrest, trust, shadow economy, access, and legitimacy.
    4. Each policy helps one system while stressing another.
    5. Survive long enough to print the final ledger.
    First moves Connect roads, add homes and jobs, build a power engine, then watch the Pressure Ledger before running a year.
